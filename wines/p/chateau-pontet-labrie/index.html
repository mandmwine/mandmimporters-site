Tasting Notes

Garnet red. Merlot led aromas of ripe plum, black cherry and sweet spice. Velvety, round and easy to enjoy, with gentle tannins.

Roast chicken, duck confit, veal meatballs, pasta with meat sauce.

Serve at 17 to 18°C (63 to 65°F).
