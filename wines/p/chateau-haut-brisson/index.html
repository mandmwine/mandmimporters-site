Tasting Notes

Deep purple red. Ripe black plum, cherry and mocha on the nose with a touch of vanilla. Round and generous on the palate with soft tannins and a long, spicy finish.

Food Pairing

Roast duck, veal chop, beef bourguignon, wild mushroom risotto made pareve.

Serve at 17 to 18°C (63 to 65°F). Decant young vintages for an hour.
