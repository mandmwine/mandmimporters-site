Feudi di San Gregorio Serpico

Region
Campania, Italy
Appellation
Irpinia Aglianico
Grapes
Aglianico
Color
Red
Vintages
2021

Tasting Notes

Deep ruby. Flagship Aglianico from ancient vines: black fruit, spice, tobacco and firm, noble tannins. Powerful and age worthy.

Food Pairing

Braised meats, lamb, aged cheese (with a dairy meal).

Serve at 17 to 18°C (63 to 65°F). Decant young vintages for an hour.
