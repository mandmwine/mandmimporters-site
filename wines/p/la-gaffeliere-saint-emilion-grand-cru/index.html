Tasting Notes

Deep ruby. Aromatic and refined: black cherry, raspberry, violet, lavender and a touch of mocha. Seamless and velvety, with chalky freshness and a long, sapid finish. Cellar worthy.

Food Pairing

Rack of lamb, beef tenderloin, duck breast, truffle dishes.

Serve at 17 to 18°C (63 to 65°F). Decant young vintages for an hour.
