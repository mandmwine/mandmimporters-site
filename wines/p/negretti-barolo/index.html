Tasting Notes

Garnet. Classic Barolo: rose, tar, red cherry and spice, with firm tannins and a long finish. Decant.

Food Pairing

Braised beef, truffle dishes, roast lamb.

Serve at 17 to 18°C (63 to 65°F). Decant young vintages for an hour.
