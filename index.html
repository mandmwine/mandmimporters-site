The Wine Doctor

Kosher as a standard of excellence, not a rarity

The goal was never simply to make a wine that was kosher, but to make extraordinary wines that could stand proudly alongside the finest vintages on the global stage.

Dr. Ralph Madeb works side by side with the families behind each estate, from the 28 generations of Fumanelli in Valpolicella to Alessandro Cellai in Tuscany, to bring their wines to the kosher table exactly as they were meant to taste.
